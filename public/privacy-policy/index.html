Privacy and data

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026. Rapid Video Downloader is designed to work without an account.

What we process

When you submit a public or authorized media link, the service processes that link to resolve metadata or prepare an available file. The submitted media URL is not written to the application analytics archive.

Temporary files may be created while a download is prepared. They are used for delivery or conversion and are automatically removed; the service is not a permanent media library.

Analytics and traffic signals

The app may record event type, page path, platform, format, quality, success status and a random browser visitor ID. The protected admin traffic archive may also process the request network address and derive country or city signals for reporting. These values are not shown publicly and are retained only as long as the operator's configured archive remains available.

Cookies and storage

The app may use browser local storage for download history, preferences and the random visitor ID. If optional Google Analytics is enabled by the operator, its own cookies and privacy terms also apply.

Service providers

Hosting, analytics and source platforms may process network information under their own policies. The app uses HTTPS, and standard hosting logs may exist outside the application analytics archive.

Your choices

You can clear local download history and site storage through your browser settings. You can stop using the service at any time. For a privacy question or data request, use the contact and takedown page.

Important limits

Only public or authorized media is supported. Private, login-required, paywalled and DRM-protected content is outside the intended use. This policy may be updated when the service changes.

Privacy and data

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026. Rapid Video Downloader is designed to work without an account.

What we process

When you submit a public or authorized media link, the service processes that link to resolve metadata or prepare an available file. The submitted media URL is not written to the application analytics archive.

Temporary files may be created while a download is prepared. They are used for delivery or conversion and are automatically removed; the service is not a permanent media library.

Analytics and traffic signals

The app records event type, page path, platform, format, quality, success status and a random browser visitor ID. The first-party archive does not store IP addresses or submitted media URLs. Google Analytics is currently enabled for product measurement and follows Google's own cookies and privacy terms.

Cookies and advertising

The app may use browser local storage for download history, preferences and the random visitor ID. The site currently uses Adsterra advertising placements; their scripts, cookies or similar technologies may process network information, frequency signals and ad measurement under Adsterra's policies. Consent choices apply where required by law.

Service providers

Hosting, analytics, advertising and source platforms may process network information under their own policies. The app uses HTTPS, and standard hosting logs may exist outside the application analytics archive.

Your choices

You can clear local download history and site storage through your browser settings. You can stop using the service at any time. For a privacy question or data request, use the contact and takedown page.

Important limits

Only public or authorized media is supported. Private, login-required, paywalled and DRM-protected content is outside the intended use. This policy may be updated when the service changes.

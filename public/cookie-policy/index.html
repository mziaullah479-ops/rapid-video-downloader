Browser storage and cookies

Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026. The downloader is designed to work without an account.

Local storage

The app may use browser local storage for download history, preferences and a random visitor identifier. You can remove this data through your browser settings or the app's reset control.

Optional analytics

If the operator enables Google Analytics, that service may set its own cookies or similar identifiers under its own privacy terms. First-party analytics may also record anonymous events and protected traffic signals for the admin dashboard.

Advertising

Advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies for frequency capping, measurement or relevant ads. Review partner notices and use your browser controls if you do not want optional storage.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies and local storage in your browser. Blocking local storage may prevent download history and preferences from working, but direct downloading can still remain available. See the Privacy Policy for more information.
